About

I'm an AI Product Manager on o9 Solutions' AI R&D team. I don't just decide what an AI agent should do, I help build the system underneath it: the map it reasons over, the guardrails that keep it safe, and the trust a human needs to actually approve its work.

What I build

A map of a company's own setup that AI agents can actually read, an agent that can extend that setup on its own, and the tools that let the people using both trust what they see.

Before product

Three years in delivery at o9, implementing large-scale supply chain planning solutions for Fortune 500 retailers, most notably leading the replenishment planning build for Starbucks. Watching configuration go slow, fragile, and expert-bound from the inside is exactly what shapes the products I build now.

Education

How I work

Five layers I move across on every product, in order, because skipping one is usually where an agent product quietly fails.

  1. 01

    User problems

    Start from what's actually slow, fragile, or expert-bound for the person doing the work, not from what the technology can do.

  2. 02

    Product workflows

    Design the end-to-end flow a user goes through, including the parts before and after the AI touches anything.

  3. 03

    AI behavior design

    Define exactly what the agent should and shouldn't do on its own, where it must stop and ask, and how it fails.

  4. 04

    System constraints

    The graph, orchestration, and infra decisions underneath, since a product promise the system can't keep isn't a product.

  5. 05

    Enterprise deployment reality

    Rollout, stakeholder buy-in, and what it takes for a skeptical implementer to actually trust and adopt it.
