Why context matters

For example, someone asks an agent“Get me the sales number from Demand Review.”

Without the graph

The agent searches by name and finds four measures that all look right.

  • Sales
  • Sales_Adj
  • Sales_Net_v2
  • Sales_LY

It has to guess, or ask someone who knows the setup.

With the graph

The agent starts at the report and follows the relationships.

  1. ReportDemand Review
  2. Shows measureSales_Net_v2
  3. Computed byCalc_Net_Sales

It gets the right number, and can show where it came from.
